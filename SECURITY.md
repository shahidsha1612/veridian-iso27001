# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

If you believe you've found a security vulnerability affecting Veridian
Identity's systems, please report it to us before disclosing it publicly.

Email: security@veridian.dev (placeholder, update once a real inbox exists)

Please include:
- A description of the issue and its potential impact
- Steps to reproduce, or a proof of concept
- Any relevant logs, screenshots, or request/response samples

We aim to acknowledge reports within 2 business days. Please do not access,
modify, or exfiltrate data belonging to other users while investigating an
issue; a synthetic account and synthetic data are sufficient to demonstrate
almost any finding.

## Scope

In scope: `veridian-verify` API and dashboard, and the infrastructure
defined in this repository. Out of scope: third-party services we
integrate with (report those to the vendor directly).
